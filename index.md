# Privacy Policy — Omniverse Labour

**Last updated: 7 October 2026**

NEVXOAULIO VENTURES ("we", "us", "our") operates the Omniverse 
Labour mobile application. This page informs you of our policies 
regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data 
when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

- **Email address**: for account authentication
- **Phone number**: for verification by contractors you work with
- **GPS location**: captured only at the moment you tap "Punch In" 
  or "Punch Out", to verify you are within the work site boundary
- **Labor ID, name, district**: for matching with project rosters

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate the labor marketplace, verify 
attendance at work sites, process wage payments, and prevent fraud.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties. 
Your phone number is masked in the app and only revealed when a 
contractor you have applied to explicitly requests it.

## Data Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. We use 
Supabase (hosted on AWS, Mumbai region) with row-level security 
policies that prevent unauthorized access.

## Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete 
Account. Your personal data (name, phone, email) is anonymized 
immediately. Attendance and wage records are kept in anonymized 
form for tax and audit requirements per Indian law.

## Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly 
collect data from children.
